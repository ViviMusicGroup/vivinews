# Help Us Test ViviMusic! 🎧

We are looking for active community testers to help test our latest APK builds, report bugs, and share feedback before public releases.

### What You'll Be Testing:
- **Audio Playback**: Background playback stability, audio cache, and offline listening.
- **Expressive UI**: New Material 3 layouts, navigation, and bottom sheets across different Android versions.
- **Performance**: Battery efficiency, memory consumption, and responsiveness.

### How to Participate:
1. Tap the **Join Telegram** button below.
2. Download the latest test APK from our Telegram channel.
3. Share your feedback, bug reports, and suggestions directly with our development team.

Thank you for helping us make ViviMusic better!
