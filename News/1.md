# 🛠️ Beta Testers Wanted!

We are looking for dedicated testers to test upcoming ViviMusic APK builds before public releases.

### 📋 Tester Requirements:
- **Must know how to capture logs**: When reporting bugs or crashes, you must be able to collect and share **Logcat** / system logs.
- **Provide clear issue reports**: Include your device model, Android version, and exact steps to reproduce the issue.
- **Active communication**: Be able to test APK updates promptly and share feedback in our Telegram group.

### 🔍 What You'll Be Testing:
- Latest features, player optimizations, and UI enhancements.
- Playback stability across various network conditions.
- Edge cases, background performance, and battery consumption.

### 🚀 How to Join:
If you meet the requirements and want to help test new builds, tap **Join Telegram** below to enter our tester group!
